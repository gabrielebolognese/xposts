Project: Xenon Night: A PWR Core, Neutron by Neutron: A full 3411 MWt pressurized-water reactor core is simulated live: two-group neutron diffusion on 18,496 cells, iodine-xenon poisoning, fuel and moderator temperature feedback, and automatic rods and boron. It runs through a night of grid load-follow and a dropped control rod, and a 6-worker Monte Carlo neutron transport run checks the eigenvalue independently.
Why it is hard: Three coupled timescales in one screen: neutron generations (microseconds, in the Monte Carlo), point kinetics after each rod step (seconds, inhour equation) and xenon poisoning (hours), all driven by one eigenvalue solver. That solver has to be correct, not just plausible: at boot it is checked against the published IAEA 2D PWR benchmark (k_eff = 1.02959). On top of that: a hand-written red-black SOR power iteration on a 136×136 two-group mesh with harmonic-mean coupling and vacuum boundaries; per-cell exact-exponential I/Xe integration; calibrated Doppler and moderator feedback; a quasi-static rod and boron controller; delta-tracking Monte Carlo spread over 6 Web Workers with Shannon-entropy source convergence. Then a 60 fps dark cockpit with a 17×17 assembly map carrying two tiny numbers per assembly, neutron tracks drawn over it, 4 field maps and 12 live charts, with no libraries.
The science: CORE MODEL: the IAEA 2D PWR benchmark (ANL-7416 Supplement 2, Problem 11), mirrored to a full core so asymmetric tilts can develop. 17×17 assemblies at a 20 cm pitch (340 cm across). Transcribe the published quarter-core region map exactly: it is diagonally symmetric, and the 'fuel 2 + rod' assemblies sit at quarter positions (1,1), (1,5), (5,1), (5,5), giving 9 rodded assemblies in the full core, which become Bank D. Corners outside the staircase boundary are void. Mesh: 2.5 cm (8×8 cells per assembly), 136×136 = 18,496 cells.
Two-group constants [D1, D2, Σ1→2, Σa1, Σa2, νΣf2] (cm, cm⁻¹; νΣf1 = 0, χ1 = 1): Fuel1 = 1.5, 0.4, 0.02, 0.01, 0.080, 0.135. Fuel2 = 1.5, 0.4, 0.02, 0.01, 0.085, 0.135. Fuel2+rod = 1.5, 0.4, 0.02, 0.01, 0.130, 0.135. Reflector = 2.0, 0.3, 0.04, 0.0, 0.010, 0. Axial buckling B² = 0.8e-4 cm⁻² in both groups.
Equations: −∇·D1∇φ1 + (Σa1 + Σ12 + D1B²)φ1 = (1/k)·νΣf2·φ2 and −∇·D2∇φ2 + (Σa2 + D2B²)φ2 = Σ12·φ1. 5-point mesh-centred finite differences. Interior face coupling = 2·Di·Dj/(Di + Dj)/h². Outer boundary from the benchmark condition −D·∂φ/∂n = 0.4692·φ, so the boundary-face leakage term is 1/(h·(h/(2D) + 1/0.4692)).
