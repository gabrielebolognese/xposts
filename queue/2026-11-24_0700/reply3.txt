3. For refactors, the tool that matters most is impact_of. It computes blast radius over CALLS, DEPENDS_ON, READS and WRITES edges. Ask your agent what breaks if you change a function, and it walks the graph instead of grepping. Since v0.8.2, blast radius also reaches past your code into Dockerfile, docker-compose and Kubernetes files. For anything custom, get_schema shows the meta-model and run_cypher runs read-only Cypher, so the agent can't change the graph through it.
