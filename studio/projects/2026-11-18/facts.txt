Project: Reentry: A Heart Stops and Restarts: A post-infarct human heart slice simulated cell by cell on the GPU (Aliev–Panfilov excitable media plus an event-driven Purkinje tree): a single premature beat at the scar border induces a spiral wave and ventricular tachycardia, a shock defibrillates it, and the 12-lead ECG is computed live from the tissue's own currents.
Why it is hard: Everything is written by hand with no libraries: a WebGL2 float-texture reaction-diffusion solver with diffusion that varies across the tissue (atria, AV node, myocardium, scar border zone, dead scar) running about 27 substeps per frame on a 512² grid. It is coupled both ways to a CPU Purkinje network that fires on events. A pseudo-ECG comes from lead fields that are exact discrete adjoints of the gradient, giving 9 electrodes and 12 leads. A real ECG measurement pipeline (Pan–Tompkins R detection, QRS/QT/QTc, frontal axis) runs on that signal. Phase-singularity detection uses topological charge, and local activation time, APD and conduction-velocity maps are built on the GPU. A 1D-cable S1–S2 restitution lab runs in a Web Worker. The arrhythmia cannot be faked: the model must be tuned so that a real S2 in the vulnerable window actually causes unidirectional block and reentry around the scar, and a real field shock actually ends it, on cue for the camera, while every panel stays dense, aligned and moving on an integrated Radeon GPU.
The science: MODEL (monodomain Aliev–Panfilov 1996), per cell on a 512×512 grid (WebGL2, RGBA32F ping-pong textures, EXT_color_buffer_float):
∂u/∂t = ∇·(D(x)∇u) − k·u·(u−a)·(u−1) − u·v + I_stim
∂v/∂t = ε(u,v)·(−v − k·u·(u−a−1)),  ε = ε0·h(x) + μ1·v/(u+μ2)
Parameters: k=8, a=0.15, ε0=0.002, μ1=0.2, μ2=0.3. Discretisation: forward Euler, dt=0.02 model units, dx=0.3, 5-point Laplacian written in flux form, ∇·(D∇u) ≈ Σ_nbr D_face(u_nbr−u)/dx² with D_face = harmonic mean of the two D values, so the scar and the outside of the heart are no-flux (D=0). Stability check: dt ≤ dx²/(4·Dmax) = 0.0225 when Dmax=1. Calibration: t_ms = 12.9·t, Vm[mV] = 100·u − 80. Playback runs at 0.5× real time (≈415 sim ms per wall second → about 27 substeps per frame at 60 fps ≈ 7.1 M cell updates per frame). Shown as measured cell updates per second.
ANATOMY (procedural four-chamber long-axis slice, a 'tissue' RGBA texture: D, h, type, scar): RA and LA thin walls (D=1). A fibrous AV annulus with D=0 isolates the atria from the ventricles except at the AV node bridge (D=0.08, which gives a PR delay of about 150 ms). LV and RV walls plus the septum (D=1). A transmural heterogeneity h(x) (epicardium h=1.35, endocardium h=1.0) shortens epicardial APD so the T wave is upright. An anterolateral LV infarct: a dense scar (D=0, non-excitable) ringed by a border zone (D=0.25, ε0×1.2) with one surviving isthmus channel.
PACING AND CONDUCTION SYSTEM: the SA node sits in the high RA and delivers I_stim=0.5 to a 6-cell disc for 1
