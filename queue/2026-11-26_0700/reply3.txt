Starting fresh? cto init creates a small CLAUDE.md, a .claudeignore, reference files in .claude/ (COMMON_MISTAKES.md, QUICK_START.md, ARCHITECTURE_MAP.md) and a docs/ folder for on-demand material. It detects your framework from files like package.json, go.mod or requirements.txt. A fresh init measures about 706 startup tokens. Careful: if you have no CLAUDE.md yet, it replaces any existing .claudeignore and markdown files in .claude/.
