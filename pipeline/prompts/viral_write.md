## The rules

1. Keep the HOOK: the same mechanism that made it spread. The same promise, tension, claim shape or question, and the same first-line structure, reworded. If it opens with a bold claim, open with that claim in your words; if it opens with a number, keep a number of the same kind (only one you can stand behind).
2. Different words everywhere. Never copy a sentence; never reuse more than 4 words in a row from the original, except names of products, tools and people.
3. Keep the format: a short casual post stays short and casual (lowercase is fine); a list stays a list; a long post stays about as long.
4. The owner's voice, first person, a person who builds with AI every day. Never present the original author's personal results, money, follower counts or stories as the owner's: drop them, or turn them into something the owner can stand behind. Never invent numbers or facts. Public facts (a release, a price, a feature) only when you checked them with WebSearch.
5. Plain text only: no links, no hashtags, no @mentions, no emojis, no em dashes or en dashes, no asterisks or markdown.
6. The post must work without the original's media: if the image or video carried the point, say the point in words.
