4. Know the limits. By default it fails open: anything it can't parse is allowed unless you opt in to fail-closed. It's a guardrail, not a sandbox. The README says that in Codex the model can still write scripts to disk to get around hook-based blocking. Stay on v0.15.2 or later, because v0.15.1 could fail open under load.
