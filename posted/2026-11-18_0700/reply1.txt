codebase-memory-mcp is an MCP server written in C. It indexes a repo into a persistent local knowledge graph of code, docs, config and infrastructure. On the Linux kernel, 28M lines and 75K files become 4.81M nodes and 7.72M edges. A full index of Django takes about 6 seconds.
