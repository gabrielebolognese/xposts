4. What it costs. The code is MIT. GitHub's runners are free for public repos, so you only pay for the model, through your Claude subscription or an API key. That came to about $0.15 to $0.55 per phone in the documented runs. Private repos use your own Actions minutes, and macOS minutes count ten times. Any Anthropic-compatible model works: Claude Haiku, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM, MiniMax or Qwen.
