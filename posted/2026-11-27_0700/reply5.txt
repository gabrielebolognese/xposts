5. Exploring is just the default. A run can also follow one feature end to end, reproduce a bug report, write an improvement report, compare two versions, or test a website on phones. v0.2.0 (released 2026-11-25) added a local dashboard that puts every screen on one map with each tester's path. Open it with: python3 scripts/dashboard.py runs/<id>. It runs on your machine and the results never leave it. The map is Android only and experimental.
