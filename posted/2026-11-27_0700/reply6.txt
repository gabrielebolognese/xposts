6. Know the limits. Findings are only as good as the model's reading of screenshots: 3 of LocalSend's 20 agents flagged the Simulator's English keyboard as part of the app. Runs are not deterministic, so expect the same kinds of bugs, not the same list. On plans with a 5-hour usage limit, 20 agents can use it up in about 15 minutes, so run batches of 6-10. Reports are encrypted with your key before they leave the runner, so only you can decrypt them.
