Project: Photon Ring: Sgr A*, Ray by Ray: A spinning Kerr black hole with Sgr A*'s mass and distance is ray-traced photon by photon on the GPU: exact null geodesics light up a relativistic thin accretion disk with a hot spot flaring around it. A virtual Event Horizon Telescope then observes the image through the night, and the shadow is checked against the analytic critical curve.
Why it is hard: Claude has to write a Kerr null-geodesic integrator from scratch inside a WebGL2 fragment shader, in Mino time with the second-order radial and polar equations so turning points need no special handling. It must detect equatorial crossings, count photon-ring orders, and accumulate coordinate time for light-travel delays, all in fp32 without blowing up near the horizon. On top of that come the Page–Thorne disk flux with its cubic-root logarithms, closed-form redshift factors, a time-delayed hot spot, and relativistic spectra and line profiles built from read-back float textures. A real EHT array follows: ECEF station coordinates, Earth-rotation uv tracks with elevation limits, visibility DFTs and a dirty image in Web Workers. Two more workers bisect the numerical critical curve and compare it with Bardeen's analytic curve. Every number on screen comes out of that physics, and everything has to hold 60 fps on an integrated Radeon.
The science: UNITS: G=c=M=1 (lengths and times in M). Physical scale is Sgr A*: M=4.297e6 Msun and D=8.277 kpc (GRAVITY 2019), so r_g = GM/c² = 6.345e9 m, θ_g = r_g/D = 5.124 µas per M, and GM/c³ = 21.17 s per M. Spin a is a slider (default 0.94) and inclination i is a slider (default 72°).

KERR CONSTANTS: Σ = r² + a²cos²θ, Δ = r² − 2r + a². Horizon r+ = 1 + √(1−a²). Prograde ISCO: Z1 = 1 + (1−a²)^{1/3}[(1+a)^{1/3} + (1−a)^{1/3}], Z2 = √(3a² + Z1²), r_isco = 3 + Z2 − √((3−Z1)(3+Z1+2Z2)). Prograde and retrograde photon orbits: r_ph± = 2[1 + cos((2/3)·arccos(∓a))]. Keplerian Ω(r) = 1/(r^{3/2} + a). Binding energy E_isco = (r^{3/2} − 2r^{1/2} + a)/(r^{3/4}√(r^{3/2} − 3r^{1/2} + 2a)) at r = r_isco, and radiative efficiency η = 1 − E_isco.

RAY TRACING (GPU fragment shader, backward from the observer): observer at r0 = 1000, θ0 = i. Pixel → Bardeen image coordinates (α, β), with the hero spanning α ∈ ±20 M and β ∈ ±16.4 M (205 × 168 µas). Constants of motion: λ = −α·sin θ0 and η = (α² − a²)cos²θ0 + β².
Radial potential R(r) = (r² + a² − aλ)² − Δ[η + (λ−a)²]. Polar potential Θ(θ) = η + a²cos²θ − λ²cot²θ.
Integrate in Mino time σ with the second-order system (no sign flips at turning points):
- d²r/dσ² = R'(r)/2, with R' = 4r(r² + a² − aλ) − (2r − 2)(η + (λ−a)²)
- d²θ/dσ² = Θ'(θ)/2, with Θ' = −2a²·sinθ·cosθ + 2λ²·cosθ/sin³θ
- dφ/dσ = (a/Δ)(r² + a² − aλ) − a + λ/sin²θ
- dt/dσ = ((r² + a²)/Δ)(r² + a² − aλ) + a(λ − a·sin²θ)
Initial velocities: v_r = −√R(r0) (inward) and v_θ = sign(β)·√Θ(θ0). RK4 w
