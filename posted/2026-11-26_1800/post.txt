people who let claude code work while they sleep
Neither of my builds got it right the first time.

Every night Claude Code builds one complex project on my PC with nobody awake. A separate Opus critic scores frames of the result. 8/10 to pass, at most 2 fix rounds.

I set up those 2 fix rounds as a safety net. I assumed it would rarely get used.

Then I pulled the numbers on my last two builds. One is a GPU heart simulation where a single premature beat sets off a spiral wave.

The other is a chess engine written from scratch. It reviews Fischer's famous queen sacrifice move by move, with its search drawn live across 8 Web Workers.

Both cleared the bar. How they got there changed how I plan every overnight run.

If you size your budget, your time window or your quality bar around the first attempt, you may be planning for a version that never ships.

There's also a line in the article about my critic that I didn't expect to write. It's about what a higher score actually buys you once it's over the bar.
