6. Caveats before you install. The savings are not from a published, repeatable benchmark, so your traffic will vary. Images are not compressed, and code in the last 4 messages is never compressed. An anonymous telemetry beacon is on by default (no prompts or code). Turn it off with HEADROOM_BEACON=off or DO_NOT_TRACK=1. The repo is open source under Apache 2.0, but a contact-only Enterprise edition exists and the README says the bigger savings are being built there.
