1. Agents with no design guidance keep shipping the same tells: Inter for everything, purple-to-blue gradients, cards nested in cards. Impeccable is an open-source skill by Paul Bakaus (Apache-2.0, about 116k GitHub stars) that gives your agent named design moves. Instead of "make it pop", you type /impeccable bolder the pricing cards. That loads a playbook written for that one move and points it at one target.
