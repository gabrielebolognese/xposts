1. opencodex is a local gateway between your coding tool and your model provider. It accepts 3 API formats: Responses, Chat Completions and Anthropic Messages. It translates each request into whatever your provider speaks. Everything runs on your machine on localhost:10100. You keep using codex the way you do now and only swap the model underneath: Claude, Gemini, Grok, GLM, DeepSeek, Kimi, Qwen or a local Ollama model.
